Our mission

Student Council exists to represent the student body to school administration and to build a school year worth remembering. We plan events, organize service work, manage class funds, and make sure student opinion is in the room when decisions get made.

How it works

01 Officers President, vice president, secretary, treasurer. Elected school-wide each spring and responsible for running meetings and school-wide events.
02 Class councils Each grade elects its own four officers. They plan what belongs to their class: senior traditions, junior fundraisers, freshman orientation.
03 Representatives Two reps per grade, elected in the fall. They bring their classmates' concerns into the room every Tuesday.
