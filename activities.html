Activities

The stuff we put on.

Spirit weeks, service drives, and the traditions that make a year at Hoggard feel like something.

Fall Homecoming week Five theme days, hall decorating by grade, the parade lineup, and the dance on Friday night.
Fall Canned food drive Collection bins in every first-period classroom. The winning grade picks the next spirit day theme.
Winter Coats for the Coast A coat and blanket drive with shelters around Wilmington. Drop-off runs through December.
Winter Exam week care packs Snacks and supplies handed out before midterms, funded by the fall fundraiser.
Spring Teacher appreciation Breakfast, notes from every grade, and the staff superlatives at the spring assembly.
Year round Viking Voice An open suggestion box for anything you want changed. We take the top items to administration each month.
Bring us your idea. That's how all of this starts.
