Activities

The stuff we put on.

Spirit weeks, service drives, and the traditions that make a year at Hoggard feel like something.

Photo
Voted by members Spirit Week Student Council is known for the amazing themes members get to vote on for each spirit week. We're always looking for fun new ideas, so make sure to attend meetings and have your voice heard.
Photo
Once a year Club Fair Once a year, Student Council hosts a club fair in Hoggard's forum as a way to get more students involved in the various clubs around campus.
Photo
Annual fundraiser Candy Cane Grams An annual fundraising event where students can send their peers candy canes and a nice note. Members are involved through donations, advertising, assembling, and delivery.
Photo
Founded by Julian Hill Planting Initiative Founded by former Student Body President Julian Hill, this initiative aims to beautify our campus. Student Council is passionate about keeping our grounds clean and full of plant life.
Photo
Homecoming week Powderpuff Every year, Student Council adds more school spirit to Homecoming week by hosting a powderpuff game between the junior and senior girls. See HOCO info →
Photo
Since 2023 Culture Fair In 2023, Hoggard hosted its first Culture Fair to highlight the diversity of the school's student body.
