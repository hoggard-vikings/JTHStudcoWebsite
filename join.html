Get involved

No experience needed.

You don't need a perfect GPA or a friend on council. You need to show up and care what happens here.

How to join

If you are a student at Hoggard, you can join Student Council anytime throughout the school year.

There are no GPA requirements for membership.

01 Come to a meeting Tuesdays at 3:15 in Room 214. No sign-up and no commitment. Sit in as many as you want.
02 Pick up an application Class representative forms are in the front office and due September 5. Officer elections happen in the spring.
03 Get elected by your grade Reps are voted in by their own class during the second week of school. Freshman elections run in October.
Applications are in the front office. Due September 5. Turn it in to Room 214 or any officer. Ask us a question

Viking Spirit Initiative

Photo
The Viking Spirit Initiative offers Student Council members an opportunity to gain hours by going to less popular sporting events. Every sport deserves an audience and we want to show some school spirit! Gain 1 hour by attending the game and an extra 30 minutes for making a sign. You MUST provide photo evidence and submit your hours form within 1 week of the event.
